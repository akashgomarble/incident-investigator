# Incident investigation report: INC-005
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The transfer failures are caused by payment-service's expired outbound mTLS client certificate (CN=payments-client.orangeinvest.internal, notAfter=2026-04-02T12:15:00Z) to bank-partner-api, not by the timeout config change, any deploy, a shared-component fault, or a partner-side outage (H1, confidence 0.70, medium). Onset is exact: bank_api_error_rate steps from ~0.2% to exactly 100.0% in the 12:15:00Z bucket, matches the cert notAfter, and was predicted 25 minutes in advance by a cert-watcher WARN, while payment_success_rate collapses from ~99% to ~2.5% with request_rate unchanged (~100 req/s). The HTTP error-rate panel is green because payment-service still returns 2xx and marks transfers PENDING_RETRY, hiding the total failure of the bank leg. All 35 buckets through 13:57Z remain pinned at 100% bank error with every logged handshake rejected and no new certificate appearing, indicating the cert expired and was never renewed. Alternatives are rejected by mechanism, timing and direct telemetry: no config/deploy change coincides with onset, cfg-2502 is a timeout key, and kafka/ledger-db telemetry is flat and normal.

## Probable root cause
**Expired mTLS client certificate on payment-service → bank-partner-api** -- credential_expiry in `payment-service`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.70, medium)

### H1: Expired mTLS client certificate on payment-service → bank-partner-api
payment-service's outbound mTLS client certificate (CN=payments-client.orangeinvest.internal) expired at exactly 2026-04-02T12:15:00Z (notAfter). Every call to bank-partner-api is rejected at the TLS handshake ('remote error: tls: expired certificate, alert 45'), so transfers stop completing (payment_success_rate ~99% → ~2-3%) while payment-service still accepts requests and returns 2xx (marking transfers PENDING_RETRY), leaving the HTTP error-rate panel green. The fault origin is payment-service's credential lifecycle (no renewal automation / no rotation executed before or after expiry), not any deploy or config change.

**Causal chain**
1. No cert rotation occurred; the client cert's notAfter silently reached 2026-04-02T12:15:00Z; a cert-watcher WARN at 11:50:26Z predicted it 25 min ahead [F10, F11, F18]
2. Cert expiry instantly breaks the mTLS handshake to bank-partner-api: 'remote error: tls: expired certificate (alert 45)'; every handshake logged is rejected, zero successes [F10, F29, F12]
3. All bank-partner-api calls fail → bank_api_error_rate jumps to exactly 100% at the 12:15Z bucket and stays pinned there [F7, F25]
4. Transfers are marked PENDING_RETRY instead of returning errors → payment_success_rate collapses from ~99% to ~2-3% and never recovers [F8, F26, F10, F27]
5. payment-service still returns HTTP 2xx to api-gateway, so HTTP error_rate stays flat ~0.1-0.32% with unchanged request_rate — the dashboard is green [F9, F27]
6. Customers experience failed fund transfers; support raises ~40 tickets; onset (12:15Z) precedes the 12:32Z report [F10, F8]

Longer timeouts tie up payment-service workers on a stalled bank leg, so transfers hang/queue instead of failing fast; async completion collapses while HTTP responses remain 2xx — hence the green error-rate panel and the ~1h gap to the 12:15Z collapse.

1. bank-partner-api began degrading; partner advisory prompts cfg-2502 raising bank.api.timeout_ms 3000→5000 at 11:20Z [F3]

3. Transfer completion collapses while HTTP responses stay 2xx and error_rate stays flat [F8, F9]
