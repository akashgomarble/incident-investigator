# Incident investigation report: INC-005
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_gave_no_actionable_requests*

## Summary
Root cause identified (high confidence, ~0.80): the mTLS client certificate CN=payments-client.orangeinvest.internal used by payment-service for outbound calls to bank-partner-api expired at 2026-04-02T12:15:00Z, so every transfer submission to the bank fails the TLS handshake from that instant and ~97-98% of payments never complete. The failures are parked in PENDING_RETRY instead of surfacing as synchronous HTTP 5xx, which is why the human-facing error_rate panel stayed green (mean 0.21%) while bank_api_error_rate sat at exactly 100% for all 105 buckets and payment_success_rate collapsed to ~2.5%. Two lesser hypotheses (bank-partner-api partner-side TLS/trust change, and the 11:20Z bank.api.timeout_ms config bump) are unsupported: the untruncated alert-45 chain names only our own client cert as the expired leaf, with no partner server/intermediate CN, and the observed error class is certificate expiry rather than timeout. Same-class precedent HIST-106 (settlement-service client cert expired, no renewal automation) supports this failure mode.

## Probable root cause

### H1: payment-service mTLS client certificate expired at 12:15Z, breaking the bank-partner-api edge
The client certificate CN=payments-client.orangeinvest.internal used by payment-service for mTLS to bank-partner-api expired at 2026-04-02T12:15:00Z (notAfter). From that instant every outbound call to bank-partner-api fails the TLS handshake, so ~100% of transfers cannot be submitted to the bank. The failures are parked in state PENDING_RETRY rather than surfaced as synchronous HTTP 5xx, so the aggregate HTTP error_rate panel stays green while bank_api_error_rate goes to 100% and payment_success_rate collapses.

**Causal chain**
1. payment-service holds a client cert CN=payments-client.orangeinvest.internal whose notAfter is exactly 2026-04-02T12:15:00Z; no renewal automation ran (HIST-106 is the same failure mode on sibling settlement-service, 'no renewal automation'). [F7, F11, F17, F21]
2. At/after 12:15:00Z every mTLS handshake from payment-service to bank-partner-api is rejected; first ERROR 12:15:27Z, 140 'mTLS handshake rejected' entries plus 171 'tls: expired certificate (alert 45)' entries. [F7, F11]
3. The full untruncated alert-45 chain (7 ERRORs, 12:16:44Z-12:20:31Z) names OUR client certificate as the expired leaf in every entry: 'our client cert CN=payments-client.orangeinvest.internal notAfter=2026-04-02T12:15:00Z'; no bank-partner-api server or intermediate cert CN appears in any alert-45 payload. [F23, F27]
4. The earliest signature is caller-side rejection: 7 untruncated 'payment <n> marked PENDING_RETRY: mTLS handshake with bank-partner-api rejected' entries, first 12:15:27Z, last 12:18:19Z. [F24]
5. bank-partner-api calls fail at 100% for the entire window (bank_api_error_rate = 100.0% on 105/105 buckets 12:15-13:55Z). [F9]
6. Payments cannot complete: payment_success_rate collapses to ~2.48% mean (1.44-3.5%). [F9]
