# Incident investigation report: INC-007
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **inconclusive** | ended: critic_inconclusive*

## Summary
The incident is narrowed to two verified auth-service change events that bracket an unambiguous step change at 14:00Z: the dep-5710 deploy (jjwt 0.11->0.12, TokenValidator rewrite) at 14:00:00Z and the cfg-2705 signing-key rotation (jwt.signing.key_id k-2025-10 -> k-2026-04) at 14:01:00Z. Onset, mechanism class and scope are well established: error_rate jumps from ~0.2% to a flat ~36% invalid_token plateau, served in-band at flat p99 (~90ms), flat request rate (~400 req/s), flat CPU/mem, zero restarts and unchanged rate-limiting, with blast radius confined to auth-service and shared-cache/capacity origins positively excluded. However, H1 (jjwt parser regression) and H2 (non-atomic key-id/JWKS propagation) cannot be separated: they have different fixes and each rests on an unverified mechanism hop, with confidence 0.50 vs 0.22 and a ranking that the Critic judged under-determined by precedent matching alone. Since every decisive discriminator (per-replica/AZ error rate, gateway 401 counters, pod-level JWKS/key-id logs, rollout/JWKS propagation metadata) is unavailable or unqueryable, I report inconclusive and treat H2 as a live alternative of comparable mechanistic weight rather than as a distant second.

## Probable root cause
**Inconclusive** -- the evidence does not single out one cause. See ranked hypotheses and data gaps.

### H1: JWT library 0.11→0.12 parser regression (dep-5710) rejects a token-shape subset
auth-service v7.3.0 (dep-5710, live at 14:00:00Z) upgraded jjwt 0.11→0.12 — a documented breaking parser API change — and rewrote TokenValidator. The new parser rejects a stable subset of otherwise-valid tokens (changed claims/clock-skew defaults, as in HIST-109), giving the persistent ~36% invalid_token plateau. The scheduled key-id rotation one minute later is a same-window coincidence, not the cause.

**Causal chain**
1. Deploy dep-5710 ships jjwt 0.12 (breaking parser API) + rewritten TokenValidator at 14:00:00Z; it is the only auth-service deployment that day and it precedes/straddles the first elevated 14:00Z bucket [F1, F13, F14, F33]
2. The rewritten parser's changed defaults reject a stable subset of token shapes → invalid_token (no direct observation of a failing token-shape cluster exists; flagged as an evidence-free hop) *(assumed)*
3. Error rate steps from ~0.20% to 22-38% in the 14:00Z bucket and holds a ~36% plateau through 15:52-15:59Z [F7, F22]
4. Failures are in-band: flat ~400 req/s, flat ~90ms p99, flat ~38% CPU / ~52% mem, flat rate limiting → validation-logic fault, not load or shedding [F8, F9, F10, F12, F17, F23]
5. The plateau is FLAT for ~2h (no decay), which is what a persistent code-path rejection produces and is in tension with a lagging key-load story that should decay [F7, F22]
6. Partial 36% (not ~100%) failure ⇒ a subset of tokens/code paths rather than a total parser break [F18]
7. Upstream api-gateway callers surface the 401s to users as logged-out sessions *(assumed)*

### H2: Scheduled signing-key rotation (cfg-2705) published the new key-id before all validators accepted it

Per precedent HIST-108, the new key-id was published before all auth-service validators loaded the new JWKS, so not-yet-updated validators (or tokens minted under the retired key-id) reject ~36% of validations. The jjwt upgrade one minute earlier is a coincidental same-window deploy, not the cause.

## What would change this conclusion
