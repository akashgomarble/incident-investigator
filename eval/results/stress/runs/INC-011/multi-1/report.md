# Incident investigation report: INC-011
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: budget_exhausted:deadline*

## Summary
The active pricing-engine config change cfg-3120 — ramping flags.pricing.greeks_v2.rollout_pct from 5% to 100% at 12:04:00Z — is the identified root cause of the order-service quote timeouts, at medium confidence (0.60). The chain is temporally tight and mechanism-specific: pricing-engine CPU stepped from ~40% to ~99-100% and p99 from ~60ms to ~2.5-2.8s only in the minute bucket following the ramp, with GreeksV2-named WARN/ERROR logs; order-service, the synchronous caller, shows a propagated 2500ms client-side timeout with flat local CPU. The competing hypotheses are contradicted by evidence: the market-data-service protobuf deploy (dep-6111, 11:56Z) produced no latency step anywhere including in market-data-service itself, redis-cache is healthy on every exposed metric, and neither pricing-engine nor order-service request_rate shows any traffic surge. The load-bearing unmeasured hop remains that GreeksV2's Monte-Carlo calculator is materially more expensive per quote than v1; that assumption plus unresolved change-management/log telemetry keeps confidence at medium rather than high.

## Probable root cause
**Greeks v2 feature-flag ramp to 100% makes every quote run the expensive Monte-Carlo path, saturating pricing-engine CPU and timing out the quote path** -- config_change in `pricing-engine`, triggered by `cfg-3120` (confidence 0.60, medium)

### H1: Greeks v2 feature-flag ramp to 100% makes every quote run the expensive Monte-Carlo path, saturating pricing-engine CPU and timing out the quote path
cfg-3120 ramped pricing-engine flags.pricing.greeks_v2.rollout_pct from 5% to 100% at 12:04:00Z. The GreeksV2 Monte-Carlo calculator (20000 paths) is far more expensive per quote than the v1 path; once it applies to all quote traffic, pricing-engine CPU saturates at ~99-100%, per-request compute exceeds the internal 2000ms deadline and the caller's 2500ms budget, so pricing-engine quote calls time out. order-service, which calls pricing-engine synchronously at its quote step, surfaces those as client-side 'timed out after 2500ms' errors and ~8% error_rate with flat local CPU. The fault originates in the pricing-engine config change, not in any code release.

2.
