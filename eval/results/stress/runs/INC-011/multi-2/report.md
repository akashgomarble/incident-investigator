# Incident investigation report: INC-011
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The order-service quote timeouts are caused by the pricing-engine config change cfg-3120, which ramped the Greeks v2 experiment flag (flags.pricing.greeks_v2.rollout_pct) from 5% to 100% at 2026-05-20T12:04:00Z (confidence 0.82, high). The flag moved the entire quote traffic mix onto the heavy GreeksV2 Monte-Carlo computation (paths=20000 per symbol), sending pricing-engine CPU from ~40% to ~99-100% and p99 from ~60ms to ~1.3-2.6s within the same 12:02-12:06 bucket, with the calculator repeatedly exceeding its own 2000ms budget. order-service, which calls pricing-engine synchronously, then exceeded its client timeout starting at 12:06:10Z (~2 minutes later), producing 225 'pricing-engine quote call timed out' errors that persist to 13:58. Alternatives were tested and contradicted: the market-data-service protobuf deploy is not on the failing hop and pre-dates onset, redis-cache is flat on every measurable metric, and request_rate/memory were flat, excluding a load- or memory-driven origin. No revert with recovery was ever observed because the flag was never rolled back, so residual uncertainty is confined to mechanism attribution, not the fix.

## Probable root cause
**pricing-engine greeks_v2 flag ramp to 100% makes the quote path CPU-bound and blows the 2000ms budget** -- config_change in `pricing-engine`, triggered by `cfg-3120` (confidence 0.82, high)
