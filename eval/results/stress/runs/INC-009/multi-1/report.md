# Incident investigation report: INC-009
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause is identified with high confidence (0.78): portfolio-service v4.5.0 (dep-5905, live since 08:56Z) shipped a new trades query filtered by account_id + executed_at with no bundled index migration, generating full sequential scans on the 48.2M-row trades table inside the shared ledger-db. That saturated ledger-db CPU (95-98%) and roughly doubled active connections in the 08:54-08:58Z window, starving order-service's INSERT INTO orders transactions until they hit statement timeouts, driving order-service p99 from ~150ms to >5s. Portfolio-service's own latency stepped ~37x (flat ~169-193ms to ~6.7-7.4s at 08:58Z) with flat request_rate and flat error_rate - the signature of a per-request query cost regression at the origin, not a load increase - and order-service's first timeout at 09:00:42Z lags it as predicted. All alternatives were tested and contradicted: the 08:00Z autovacuum change (no vacuum/analyze on trades in-window, wrong direction, 54-min unexplained gap), infrastructure exhaustion (disk and replication-lag proxies flat; no writer shows a load step), and organic aggregate load growth (payment and settlement services flat at onset). Residual uncertainty comes from two unqueryable premises carried as assumptions: the absence of a supporting index and the application_name-only attribution of the Seq Scans.

## Probable root cause
**portfolio-service v4.5.0 unindexed trades query saturates shared ledger-db, back-pressuring order-service** -- bad_deployment in `portfolio-service`, triggered by `dep-5905` (confidence 0.78, high)

### H1: portfolio-service v4.5.0 unindexed trades query saturates shared ledger-db, back-pressuring order-service
portfolio-service v4.5.0 (dep-5905, 08:56Z) added a new query on the large `trades` table (48.2M rows) filtered by account_id + executed_at with no bundled migration/index. This produced full sequential scans (slow queries logged on ledger-db with application_name=portfolio-service, rows=48211907), driving ledger-db CPU to ~95-98% and roughly doubling active connections, which starved order-service's INSERT INTO orders transactions until they hit the statement timeout, giving p99 >5s. Category: bad_deployment because the fault originates in the code shipped by dep-5905.
