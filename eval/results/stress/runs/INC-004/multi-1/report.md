# Incident investigation report: INC-004
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
notification-service's near-daily OOMKills (exit 137) are caused by the unbounded in-memory TemplateCache introduced in v3.8.0 (2026-03-21T00:00Z), which never evicts and grows monotonically (~50k -> ~190-197k entries) within each ~22-23h cycle until old-gen heap saturates and the kubelet kills the pod. Confidence is medium (0.70, capped by the Critic): the cache size resets exactly at each restart in lockstep with the container memory sawtooth, and the GC-exhaustion-then-exit-137 signature recurs on 03-21/03-22/03-23 independent of the 03-23 Kafka maintenance. The reporter's Kafka-replay theory and the alternative config/upstream/limit-reduction hypotheses are actively contradicted by verified findings (no replay/rebalance/deserialization errors; lag spikes coincide with rather than precede the OOM; no config change touching notification-service limits). Two residuals keep this short of certainty: whether OOMs predate the deploy is unrecoverable (telemetry starts 03-20), and byte-level heap attribution to the cache is unmeasurable, so the cache's majority-of-heap share rests on correlation with mild counter-evidence in the non-constant entry-count/memory ratio.

## Probable root cause
**notification-service v3.8.0's unbounded in-memory TemplateCache leaks heap and OOMs the pod** -- bad_deployment in `notification-service`, triggered by `dep notif v3.8.0 2026-03-21T00:00:00Z` (confidence 0.70, medium)

### H1: notification-service v3.8.0's unbounded in-memory TemplateCache leaks heap and OOMs the pod
notification-service v3.8.0 (2026-03-21T00:00Z) added an in-memory TemplateCache (ConcurrentHashMap keyed by template+locale+userSegment) with no eviction bound. Distinct keys are retained indefinitely, so heap grows steadily with traffic; over ~22h the heap crosses the container limit, producing escalating old-gen full GCs, and the kubelet OOMKills the pod (exit 137). The restart clears the cache, memory drops back to ~46-48%, and the cycle repeats roughly daily. The reported ~25min email delay and the consumer-lag spike are consequences of the restart/backlog drain, not its cause.

**Causal chain**
1. Deployment v3.8.0 adds an unbounded in-memory TemplateCache (no eviction, evictions=0 on every log line) at 2026-03-21T00:00Z. [F1, F14, F34]
2. Cache entry count grows monotonically within each cycle, ~9k -> ~190-197k entries, never evicting. [F14, F15, F34]
3. Container memory_pct ramps steadily and linearly (~4-5%/2h) from a ~46-48% post-restart floor to 93-98% over ~22-24h (sawtooth), matching the cache refill window. [F8, F9, F16, F29]
4. Heap saturation produces escalating old-gen full-GC WARNs, ending in a kubelet OOMKill (exit 137). [F10, F13, F18, F26]
5. Memory and cache size reset together at each restart, then recur ~every 23h across 03-21/03-22/03-23. [F7, F9, F14, F15, F26]
6. The restart causes a transient consumer-lag spike and ~25min email delay while the backlog drains; lag returns to baseline within ~30min. [F11, F12]
7. Only notification-service shows the sawtooth; auth/order/settlement (sharing cluster/Kafka consumer infra) stay flat at ~49-60% with zero restarts, excluding a cluster/infra-wide cause. [F19, F20, F21, F22, F24]
