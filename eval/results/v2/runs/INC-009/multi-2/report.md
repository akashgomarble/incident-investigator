# Incident investigation report: INC-009
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified with high confidence (0.78): the portfolio-service v4.5.0 deploy dep-5905 at 2026-05-05T08:56:00Z introduced a new unindexed trades query ('SELECT * FROM trades WHERE account_id=$1 AND executed_at > $2 ORDER BY executed_at') that runs a 48.2M-row Seq Scan against the shared ledger-db, driving ledger-db CPU to ~95-97% and active connections to ~190 and starving order-service's INSERT INTO orders past statement_timeout (p99 ~5s). The change point is coincident with the deploy, not with the DBA autovacuum change cfg-2903 at 08:00:00Z, and the repeated slow query matches the deploy's declared change verbatim with application_name=portfolio-service. The reporter's autovacuum suspicion (H2) and the infrastructure/co-tenant hypotheses (H3/H4) are rejected on timing, mechanism (no lock/deadlock/IO-wait/vacuum-worker log entries in the full 392-entry ledger-db set), flat disk, and no pooler in topology. Residual uncertainty (hence the 0.78 cap rather than higher): sub-2-minute ordering between the 08:56 deploy and the 08:55-labelled metric bucket is unresolvable at 120s resolution, and H1's scope claim that shared-ledger-db saturation starves ALL direct consumers is partly contradicted by payment-service and settlement-service showing no degradation.

## Probable root cause
**portfolio-service v4.5.0 new unindexed trades query saturates shared ledger-db (bad deploy)** -- bad_deployment in `portfolio-service`, triggered by `dep-5905` (confidence 0.78, high)

### H1: portfolio-service v4.5.0 new unindexed trades query saturates shared ledger-db (bad deploy)
The dep-5905 deploy of portfolio-service v4.5.0 at 08:56:00Z introduced a new query 'SELECT * FROM trades WHERE account_id=$1 AND executed_at > $2 ORDER BY executed_at' that performs a 48.2M-row Seq Scan on the shared ledger-db. ledger-db is consumed directly by four services (order-service, portfolio-service, payment-service, settlement-service); the scan drives ledger-db CPU to ~95-97% and active connections to ~190, starving order-service's INSERT INTO orders past statement_timeout and driving its p99 to ~5s. portfolio-service degrades hardest (p99 ~7s) as it is both the scan's source and a victim. The 08:55-labelled 'onset' reported earlier is a bucket-alignment artifact: at 2-minute resolution the last healthy point is the 08:55 bucket and the first degraded point is 08:57, placing the change point at the deploy instant and inside the same window as the deploy.

**Causal chain**

### H2: DBA autovacuum scale-factor change on ledger-db causes bloat/lock-driven statement timeouts (reporter's suspicion)
cfg-2903 reduced autovacuum_vacuum_scale_factor from 0.2 to 0.1 on ledger-db at 08:00:00Z. More aggressive autovacuum triggers vacuum workers that consume IO/CPU and hold locks on trades, delaying queries and causing statement timeouts in order-service. Evidence: the config change is real, it is the only config change in the window, and it precedes the incident.

**Causal chain**
1. cfg-2903 lowers autovacuum_vacuum_scale_factor to 0.1 on ledger-db at 08:00:00Z. [F1, F13]
2. More aggressive autovacuum causes vacuum workers to run on the large trades table. *(assumed)*
3. Vacuum work consumes IO/CPU and takes locks, slowing concurrent queries. *(assumed)*
4. Slow/locked queries on ledger-db cause statement timeouts in order-service and slow portfolio-service queries. *(assumed)*
