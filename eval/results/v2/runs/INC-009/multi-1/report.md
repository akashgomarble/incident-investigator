# Incident investigation report: INC-009
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified with medium confidence (0.62): portfolio-service v4.5.0 (dep-5905, 08:56Z) shipped a new trades query with no supporting migration/index, and its Seq Scan over ~48.2M rows flooded the shared ledger-db, saturating CPU at ~95-97% and starving order-service's INSERT INTO orders into statement timeouts (p99 ~5s, ~9-10% errors). The timing is tight and correctly ordered: cfg-2903 (autovacuum, 08:00Z) precedes onset by ~55 min with unchanged autovacuum cadence, while the first ledger-db slow query at 08:58:38Z is verbatim the dep-5905 query attributed 100% to portfolio-service, and CPU/slow-query/latency all step in the same 08:50-09:00 bucket. Co-tenants payment-service and settlement-service stay flat on p99/error/pool while only order-service steps, matching a shared-datastore saturation that order-service is uniquely exposed to via connections held on blocked INSERTs, rather than a node-wide fault. Residual uncertainty is limited to unprovable endpoint-level co-residency and the CPU-starvation vs row-lock mechanism, both structurally outside available telemetry.

## Probable root cause
**portfolio-service dep-5905 shipped an unindexed 48M-row Seq Scan that saturated the shared ledger-db, starving order-service INSERTs** -- bad_deployment in `portfolio-service`, triggered by `dep-5905` (confidence 0.62, medium)

### H2: portfolio-service dep-5905 shipped an unindexed 48M-row Seq Scan that saturated the shared ledger-db, starving order-service INSERTs
portfolio-service v4.5.0 (dep-5905, 08:56Z) shipped a 'new query on trades filtered by account_id + executed_at' with no migration and therefore no supporting index. Each execution performs a Seq Scan over ~48.2M rows on the shared ledger-db, driving slow_queries from ~1/min to ~137-142/min and CPU from ~30% to ~95-97%. order-service, a co-tenant on that same datastore, has its own INSERT INTO orders starved of CPU and canceled by the statement timeout, giving p99 ~5s and ~9-10% errors, with its db_pool_in_use tripling as connections are held open on blocked statements.

**Causal chain**
1. dep-5905 deploys portfolio-service v4.5.0 at 08:56Z with a new trades query on account_id + executed_at and explicitly no migration (so no index was added) [F2, F3]
2. First portfolio-service slow query appears at 08:58:38Z — 'SELECT * FROM trades WHERE account_id=$1 AND executed_at > $2 ORDER BY executed_at -- Seq Scan on trades (rows=48211907) application_name=portfolio-service' — while slow_queries was flat ~1/min through 08:40 [F13, F15, F18, F31, F32]
3. Repeated 48M-row sequential scans step ledger-db CPU from ~30% to ~95-97% and slow_queries to ~137-142/min in the same 08:50-09:00 bucket, ~55 min after cfg-2903 and ~2 min after the deploy [F10, F11, F14, F15]
4. Co-tenancy is confirmed by differential blast radius in the SAME client set: order-service steps in p99 (~150ms->~5000ms), error_rate (~0.2%->9-10%) and db_pool_in_use (14->44-48), while payment-service and settlement-service — also ledger-db clients — stay flat on all three, exactly what identical co-residency plus one client whose pool is pinned on blocked INSERTs predicts [F7, F8, F12, F27, F28, F29, F30, F33]
5. order-service INSERT INTO orders, starved of DB resources, exceeds its statement timeout: 284 'canceling statement due to statement timeout (INSERT INTO orders)' events from 09:00:42Z, matching the p99 and error step [F9]
