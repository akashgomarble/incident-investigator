# Incident investigation report: INC-007
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **inconclusive** | ended: critic_inconclusive*

## Summary
The origin of the 401 storm is firmly localised to auth-service, but the specific root cause cannot be separated: two auth-local changes 60 seconds apart (dep-5710 jjwt 0.11->0.12 TokenValidator rewrite at 14:00:00Z and cfg-2705 jwt.signing.key_id rotation at 14:01:00Z) both bracket the clean 14:00-14:02Z step onset from ~0.2% to ~36%, and every discriminator (failure signature, per-instance blast radius, rollout mechanics, onset-decay shape) is either absent from telemetry or permanently closed. All five alternative origins are now affirmatively contradicted: redis-cache siblings are flat (F24/F29), the api-gateway's ~21% step is 71/71 relayed auth-service 401 lines with zero 5xx and flat request_rate (F36/F38/F39/F40/F41), and no gateway/client change appears in the 24h sweep (F3/F4). The reviewed ranking leaves H1 (key rotation, 0.34) and H2 (jjwt upgrade, 0.38) statistically tied at low confidence, so no root cause is claimed; H2's central hop (jjwt 0.12 changing a default that rejects a stable ~36% token subset) remains asserted, not demonstrated. Recommended approach: mitigate by reversing both changes (staged re-publication of the previous kid plus rollback of v7.3.0), then confirm the 401 rate returns to baseline and re-land one change at a time to identify the culprit.

**Inconclusive** -- the evidence does not single out one cause. See ranked hypotheses and data gaps.

### H2: JWT library upgrade (dep-5710, jjwt 0.11->0.12) regressed TokenValidator semantics

HIST-109 mechanism, fixed by rollback rather than key re-publication.
