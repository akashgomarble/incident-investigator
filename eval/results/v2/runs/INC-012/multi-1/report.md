# Incident investigation report: INC-012
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The payment failures in INC-012 were caused by capacity exhaustion of the shared ledger-db: its disk filled from 89.4% (13:00Z) to 100% by 14:58Z, and PostgreSQL could no longer write WAL or extend data files, so all write-heavy users of that datastore began failing their writes (H2, confidence 0.72, medium). The payment-service v6.0.0 mandate flow (dep-6203) is coincidental: ledger-db was already 95% full at 14:15:45Z and ~98% at the 14:46Z deploy, a writer that never received v6.0.0 (order-service) emits the identical ENOSPC signature, and the 15:12Z rollback produced no recovery in any writer through 16:57Z. The dominant error everywhere is 'No space left on device', not a validation or unknown-entry-type rejection (H1/H3 rejected at 0.02 each). Residual uncertainty is limited to the exact space consumer — WAL retained by the inactive 'analytics_cdc' replication slot is strongly indicated by recurring ledger-db WARNs and the exact HIST-114 precedent, but no per-slot byte metric exists to prove it — and the uncharacterised settlement-service error signature.

## Probable root cause
**Shared ledger-db disk exhaustion from WAL retained by an inactive replication slot — all write-heavy writers fail** -- resource_exhaustion in `ledger-db`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.72, medium)

### H2: Shared ledger-db disk exhaustion from WAL retained by an inactive replication slot — all write-heavy writers fail
ledger-db's filesystem filled steadily during the morning (89.4% at 13:00Z -> 100% by 14:58Z) while an inactive replication slot 'analytics_cdc' retained ~78 GB of WAL, and the retained WAL is the dominant space consumer. PostgreSQL reached 100% disk and could no longer write WAL or extend data files, so every write-heavy client of the shared ledger-db (payment-service, order-service, settlement-service) began failing its DB writes at ~14:54-15:01Z, surfacing as ~48-62% error rates. No faulty deployment is required, and the payment-service v6.0.0 rollout is coincidental.

**Causal chain**
1. Inactive replication slot 'analytics_cdc' retains 78423 MB of WAL, warned from 14:16:25Z onward (5 occurrences) [F11]
2. ledger-db disk_used_pct rises monotonically 89.39% (13:00Z) -> >95% by ~14:05-14:10Z -> 98%+ from the 14:38Z bucket -> 100.0% first reached in the 14:58Z bucket, then pins at exactly 100 through 16:55Z with no recovery [F19, F20]
3. PostgreSQL can no longer write: WARN 'disk usage 95%' 14:15:45Z / 'disk usage 98%' 14:48:20Z, then hard failures PANIC 'could not write to file pg_wal/xlogtemp...: No space left on device' from 15:01:01Z (153x) and 'could not extend file base/16402/2619: No space left on device' from 15:01:39Z (139x) [F10]

5. payment-service ledger writes fail: 88 of 89 matched WARN+ lines are 'ledger write failed: ERROR: could not extend file: No space left on device', first at 15:00:26Z; error rate jumps 0.2% -> 61.52% between the 14:55 and 15:00 buckets [F8, F7]

## What would change this conclusion
