# Incident investigation report: INC-003
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The order-service 504s are caused by failures on the exchange-gateway -> exchange-api (external exchange partner) hop, not by the order-service v2.15.2 release. exchange-gateway's outbound calls to host=api.exchange-partner.example began returning 5000ms connect timeouts and partner-issued 503s at 11:01:25Z, its circuit breaker opened at 11:03:26Z, and order-service's 30% submit failure rate (first error 11:02:45Z) is just the downstream symptom on that shared hop. A second, independent caller of exchange-gateway (market-data-service) degraded at the same 11:00Z onset, and nothing recovered after the 11:16Z rollback: exchange-gateway's outbound error rate held at ~45.9% with ~9.3s upstream latency through 12:55Z. Confidence is medium-high (0.68 per the reviewed ranking, capped for the irreducible ambiguity between a partner-internal fault and a degraded path between our fleet and the partner); either way the fault is external to order-service and the rollback is not the fix.

## Probable root cause
**External exchange-partner API (exchange-api) outage/degradation causes 504s on order submit** -- dependency_failure in `exchange-api (external exchange partner, reached via exchange-gateway)`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.68, medium)

### H1: External exchange-partner API (exchange-api) outage/degradation causes 504s on order submit
The order-service 504s originate outside our fleet: the external vendor api.exchange-partner.example (exchange-api) began failing/responding slowly, so exchange-gateway's outbound placeOrder/quoteSnapshot calls hit 5000ms connect timeouts and partner-issued 503s, its circuit breaker opened at 11:03:26, which surfaced as order-service 504 'exchange-gateway call failed' errors on the submit path. Rolling back order-service cannot fix it, and indeed nothing recovered after the 11:16Z rollback.

**Causal chain**
1. External partner api.exchange-partner.example degrades (outage or severe slowness/overload) at ~10:58-11:01Z; it is an external node outside all internal change records [F11, F15, F13, F3]
2. exchange-gateway's outbound calls to the partner fail: 163 placeOrder + 146 quoteSnapshot 5000ms connect timeouts AND 161 'exchange-api returned 503 Service Unavailable' responses carrying partner-issued x-partner-request-id values — i.e. the partner edge is reachable but refusing/slow for BOTH operations and the ONLY destination [F11, F19, F23]
3. exchange-gateway latency steps from ~128ms to ~5000-5600ms (timeout-shaped, not a load ramp) and error_rate rises to 43-46%, with upstream_error_rate rising identically (fault is on its outbound call, not its own logic); no node CPU/load anomaly [F10, F12, F22]
4. Circuit breaker opens for exchange-api (failure ratio 0.47 over 60s at 11:03:26), sustaining the failures [F11, F23]
5. order-service submit calls fail with '504 Gateway Timeout (placeOrder)'; p99 pins to ~5.2s against its 5s timeout; error_rate jumps to ~30% in the 11:00 bucket [F8, F9, F7]
6. A second, independent caller (market-data-service) is starved by the same exchange-gateway/exchange-api hop from the same ~11:02 onset, with no error of its own — proving the fault is downstream on the exchange edge, not in either caller [F25, F26, F27]
7. No recovery after the 11:16Z order-service rollback: exchange-gateway upstream_error_rate stays ~45.86% and upstream_latency ~9.3s through 12:55Z [F28, F29, F30]

### H5: Order-service is innocent: fault on exchange-gateway's own egress/DNS path (our infrastructure), not the partner

The failure is nonetheless external to order-service, so rolling back order-service still would not have fixed it. This is a different ORIGIN from H1 (our infrastructure vs the partner's) and implies a different fix (our network/DNS remediation vs partner escalation/failover).
