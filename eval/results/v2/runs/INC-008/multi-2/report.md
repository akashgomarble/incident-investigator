# Incident investigation report: INC-008
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified (medium confidence, 0.68, capped): auth-service capacity saturation. A ~4x login-traffic surge beginning ~06:50Z drove auth-service CPU to 96-99% while its autoscaler was capped at maxReplicas=8 (desiring 14), filling the worker pool (64/64, queue up to ~98k) and the accept queue; requests were shed as 503s and queueing pushed p99 to ~3.8s, with rate limiting engaging in the identical 06:52->06:54 onset bucket and plateauing at ~900/min for ~2h. The reporter's suspected bcrypt regression (H4) and the log.level config change (H3) are affirmatively contradicted: auth-service had zero deployments in the window and the only change was a logging verbosity flag, while the error signatures are accept-queue/worker-pool exhaustion, not hashing/DB/OOM. The one competing hypothesis naming a different origin (H2, portfolio-service v4.4.0) is contradicted on every testable leg. The remaining uncertainty is the origin of the surge — broad-external growth vs a single abusive caller — which is not decidable from the available telemetry (no caller/client dimension exists anywhere); this caps confidence but does not change the category or the immediate remedy.

### H1: Capacity saturation: ~4x login-traffic surge overwhelms auth-service whose autoscaler is capped at maxReplicas 8
A ~4x surge in login traffic beginning ~06:50Z drove auth-service's CPU and worker pool to saturation. With the autoscaler capped at maxReplicas=8 (desiring 14), capacity could not follow the backlog; the accept queue and worker pool filled, requests were shed as 503s, and queueing pushed p99 to ~3.8s. Rate limiting, previously negligible, engaged at the identical 06:52->06:54 onset bucket and stayed at a ~900/min plateau for ~2h, confirming a persistent demand-above-capacity condition rather than a short transient. No faulty code or config change is required. The precise origin of the surge (broad external ramp, bot/abuse ramp, or a single upstream caller) is not identifiable from available telemetry because no caller/client dimension exists in any metric or log series, which is the main remaining gap and the reason confidence is capped.

**Causal chain**
1. Login request rate to auth-service roughly quadruples, ~383-404 req/s -> ~1462-1612 req/s (peak 1814), beginning in the 06:50-06:52 bucket [F13, F21]
2. api-gateway's ingress rises at the same onset and by MORE in absolute terms (+~3.9k req/s vs auth's +~1.4k), so the extra load originates upstream of the gateway->auth edge rather than being replayed auth calls [F22]
3. Effective rate limiting engages at the identical onset bucket: rate_limited_per_min steps from ~1.2-2.9/min (normal through 06:52) to ~450-919/min in the 06:54 bucket, then holds a ~870-920/min plateau through 08:54, i.e. continuous shedding for ~2h [F35, F36, F39]
