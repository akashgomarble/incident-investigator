# Incident investigation report: INC-006
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified with high confidence (0.86–0.87): the shared redis-cache lost its dataset at ~13:20–13:22Z when node redis-cache-2 failed its health check and an empty, still-resyncing replica was promoted, producing a cold cache (hit_ratio_pct 96%→13.75%, memory_pct ~70%→7.25%). market-data-service then fell into a cache-miss storm and rebuilt each quote from the exchange feed snapshot (1034–8748ms), blowing pricing-engine's per-quote deadline and stepping its p99 from ~60ms to ~1.4–2.3s. This matches HIST-107 ("Cold cache storm after redis failover") service-for-service and symptom-for-symptom. Alternative origins — exchange-gateway/feed degradation, auth-service v7.2.0 cookie hardening, and pricing-engine client-pool saturation — are each directly falsified by flat exchange-leg measurements, timing/topology, and unchanged local resources respectively.

## Probable root cause
**redis-cache primary failure promoted an empty replica (cold cache) — HIST-107 repeat** -- infrastructure_failure in `redis-cache`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.86, high)

### H1: redis-cache primary failure promoted an empty replica (cold cache) — HIST-107 repeat
The shared redis-cache behind market-data-service and pricing-engine lost its dataset at ~13:20–13:22Z: node redis-cache-2 failed its health check (3 consecutive PING timeouts) and was marked primary-down at 13:22:10Z; the replica promoted at 13:22:52Z was EMPTY because it was still resyncing. That emptied the dataset, dropping hit_ratio_pct from ~96% to 13.75% and memory_pct from ~70% to 7.25%. market-data-service, whose quote lookups depend on the cache, fell into a cache-miss storm and had to rebuild each quote from the exchange feed snapshot (1.0–8.7s), which blew pricing-engine's per-quote deadline ('market-data-service took up to 8456ms'). The visible symptom is pricing-engine p99 stepping from ~60ms to ~1.4–2.3s and slow quotes on the trading screens; api-gateway inherits the same slow-but-successful path with no new 5xx. The exchange-gateway leg is measured clean, so the slow rebuilds are explained by cache loss plus the cost of refilling, not by a slow upstream feed.

**Causal chain**
