# Incident investigation report: INC-004
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The recurring OOMKill cycle in notification-service is best explained by H1 (confidence 0.72, medium): the in-memory TemplateCache introduced by dep-5402 (v3.8.0, 2026-03-21T00:00Z) accumulates heap without an evident bound, producing a ~24h sawtooth and three exit-137 restarts. The onset evidence is tight — the entire observable pre-deploy day is flat at ~47-50% (F21), the deploy bucket is the local minimum (F23), and the first sustained monotonic ramp starts in the very next bucket while CPU stays flat at ~24.9% (F22/F25/F26) — and the alternative Kafka-retention story (H2) is structurally contradicted because cfg-2410 (03-23T18:00Z) postdates two of the three OOMKills and no broker restart, reassignment, or partition/topic change is recorded (F33/F34/F35). Confidence is capped at medium because the unbounded-growth mechanism itself is asserted from a one-line deploy manifest, not from code, cardinality metadata, or heap/old-gen telemetry (F14/F15/F16/F24). Treat this as an identified origin with a named suspect change rather than a proven defect; rule out H2 empirically and verify the cache bound in code before closing.

## Probable root cause
**In-memory TemplateCache added by dep-5402 grows unbounded until recurring OOM cycle** -- bad_deployment in `notification-service`, triggered by `dep-5402` (confidence 0.72, medium)

### H1: In-memory TemplateCache added by dep-5402 grows unbounded until recurring OOM cycle
notification-service v3.8.0 (dep-5402, 2026-03-21T00:00Z, 'Add in-memory template cache for email rendering', ConcurrentHashMap keyed by template+locale+userSegment) introduced an in-memory cache inside notification-service that retains entries without an evident bound. Heap grows monotonically under steady consume load (memory_pct ramps +5-6pp per 2h bucket, CPU unchanged, old-gen pressure and multi-second full GCs) until the container hits its limit and is OOMKilled; the abrupt memory drop is the restart and the cycle repeats each ~24h. This origin is specific to notification-service: it is the ONLY service in the metric set with a sawtooth ramp/reset (F32), and the other broker-sharers are flat (settlement-service F27-F31, order-service F11), so the driver is the service's own heap growth, not shared-broker volume.

**Causal chain**
1. dep-5402 deploys notification-service v3.8.0 adding a ConcurrentHashMap template cache keyed by template+locale+userSegment; no rollback ever occurs [F1, F14, F17, F35]
2. Before the deploy the memory series is flat at ~47-50% for the entire observable pre-deploy day with no trend; the 03-21T00:00Z deploy bucket is the local MINIMUM (transient dip consistent with restart/scale at deploy time) [F21, F23]
3. The first sustained monotonically rising memory averages begin in the very first bucket after the deploy (20-min rise starts 00:20Z; 40-min first strictly-rising bucket 00:40Z), climbing unbroken to ~58.97% by 06:00Z [F22, F26]
4. Growth is accumulation, not increased processing: memory ramps while cpu_pct stays flat at ~24.4-25.3 across the deploy and the ramp [F25]
5. Old-gen heap crosses 90% and full GCs lengthen to 1.6-8.2s, clustering in the ramp/peak windows — retained entries are not reclaimed [F10]
6. memory_pct sawtooths from ~48-50% to ~97-98% then drops abruptly to ~48%, three times (resets ~21T22:00, 22T22:00, 23T20:00) [F7]
7. Each cycle ends in an OOMKill (exit 137) at 03-21T22:59:50Z, 03-22T21:59:47Z and 03-23T19:59:21Z; the restart resets the container, closing the loop [F8]
8. Scope check: NO other service in the metric set exhibits a sawtooth — settlement-service is flat (49.15-50.75 avg, zero restarts, lag max 82) and auth-service is flat (51.16-53.17 avg, zero restarts), corroborating that the accumulation is local to notification-service's own heap rather than a shared-broker/volume effect [F27, F28, F29, F30, F31, F32]
9. The very large short-lived consumer_lag spike accompanying each restart window is read as a symptom of the cycle (backlog that queued while the consumer was stalled during the ramp and around the restart), not as the driver — CPU-flat growth rules out throughput-driven growth [F9, F25]

### H2: Kafka retention reduction (cfg-2410) forced a replay/backlog surge notification-service could not absorb
The platform-team Kafka maintenance cfg-2410 (log.retention.ms 7d->3d at 2026-03-23T18:00Z) forced a replay/backlog surge dumped into notification-service, which it could not absorb, blowing up its heap and OOMKilling it ~2h20m after the change. Structural timings strongly constrain this: cfg-2410 postdates two of the three OOMKills, and the record documents no broker restart or partition reassignment, so it can at most explain the 03-23 episode via an undocumented mechanism.

**Causal chain**
1. cfg-2410 reduces Kafka log.retention.ms 7d->3d at 03-23T18:00Z — the only config change in the window [F2]
2. Retention/broker maintenance triggers a replay/backlog surge (pattern of HIST-104 / HIST-112) [F5]
3. notification-service sees a huge short-lived consumer_lag spike (03-23T18:00Z avg 810, max 18138) [F9]
4. Processing the surge drives heap to its limit and the container is OOMKilled (exit 137 at 19:59:21Z) [F8, F10]
