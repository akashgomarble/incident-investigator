# Incident investigation report: INC-007
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **inconclusive** | ended: critic_inconclusive*

## Summary
Investigators could not separate the two leading causes of INC-007: H1, the cfg-2705 signing-key rotation (jwt.signing.key_id k-2025-10 -> k-2026-04 at 14:01:00Z) leaving some pods/JWKS caches stale (confidence 0.42), and H2, the dep-5710 auth-service v7.3.0 deploy (jjwt 0.11->0.12 breaking parser API, TokenValidator rewritten at 14:00:00Z) regressing validation for a token subset (confidence 0.40, and the two are effectively a coin flip). Both are auth-service-scoped changes 60 seconds apart, both fall inside the onset bracket (F1/F42 pin onset between the 14:01 and 14:02 buckets, which cannot order a 14:00:00Z deploy against a 14:01:00Z config apply), and neither has been rolled back (F11/F35). Every discriminator attempted failed on data availability: the only reachable error text is the generic "401 invalid_token" (F27/F28/F40), kid/claim/signature keyword probes returned zero (F29), auth-service's own logs are unreachable (F25/F38/F39), and neither metrics nor the change feed expose per-reason, per-replica, JWKS-reload or rollout metadata (F26/F32/F33/F34/F43). H3 is affirmatively contradicted (F19/F20/F21/F22/F23) and H4 was correctly rejected on flat resource/latency/restart/load signals plus the clean one-bucket step (F3/F4/F5/F6/F41/F42). Because the two survivors specify different fixes (re-publish the previous kid / stage the rotation vs roll back dep-5710 / pin clock-skew), the team should ship the two-cause diagnosis with a discriminating rollout/reload probe and treat the missing telemetry as observability debt.

### H1: Signing-key rotation cfg-2705 published a new kid not all pods had loaded → stale-kid validations fail 401
cfg-2705 changed jwt.signing.key_id from k-2025-10 to k-2026-04 at 2026-04-15T14:01:00Z (author security-automation, 'Scheduled signing-key rotation'). It is the only config change across all services in the window (F14) and is scoped to auth-service (F19). Under the HIST-108 mechanism, pods/JWKS caches that had not yet loaded the new key material keep validating against the old kid, so the share of traffic hitting un-updated pods returns 401 invalid_token — a partial, flat ~36% rather than 100%. Origin: auth-service's own identity key configuration. The rotation is provably internal to auth-service (no JWKS/secret-store/identity-provider node at depth 3, F21/F22/F23; the new kid appears in exactly one auth-service-scoped record, F19). What remains unobserved and is the whole crux: whether the rejection is actually kid-driven.

**Causal chain**
1. cfg-2705 applies at 14:01:00Z, rotating jwt.signing.key_id k-2025-10 -> k-2026-04 by security-automation as a scheduled rotation; it is the only config change in the window and is auth-service-scoped [F14, F10, F19]
2. auth-service error_rate steps from 0.16-0.31% in every 1-min bucket 13:55-14:01 to 36.08% at 14:02:00Z and stays 34.4-38.53% through 14:10, so the step is between the 14:01 and 14:02 buckets — cfg-2705's 14:01:00Z apply falls inside that transition bucket [F42, F1, F2]
3. The new kid is published before all replicas/JWKS paths load it, so a persistent fraction of validations resolves an unknown/absent kid (the per-pod stale-JWKS mechanism of HIST-108) [F9, F7]
4. Those validations return 401 invalid_token, holding flat at ~35-37% for the rest of the window rather than ramping or reaching 100% [F2, F27, F28, F30, F31]
5. A pure auth-logic rejection leaves CPU, latency, memory, restarts, request rate and throttling all flat across onset [F3, F4, F5, F6, F41]

## Data gaps
- No per-replica, per-pod or per-AZ metric dimension exists for auth-service (only 7 whole-service series), so it cannot be determined whether the ~36% failure is uniform across replicas or concentrated on a stale-key subset.
- auth-service's own logs are unqueryable (backend 503) and no sidecar/log-collector/container-runtime service exists, so no validation error text (unknown kid vs signature vs clock skew vs claim/parse) could be obtained — the central discrimination is unavailable.
- No token-level or kid-level telemetry exists (no validation outcome by kid, token issuance time, or issuer), so the population split implied by H1 cannot be measured or refuted.
- The change feed exposes no rollout strategy, percent-of-pods-updated, rollout completion time or skew/leeway fields for any record, so it cannot be established whether cfg-2705 was a staged canary or whether dep-5710 was rolling/canary.
- Historical records return only title/symptoms/root_cause/resolution and never state the observed failure percentage for HIST-108 or HIST-109, so the ~1/3 partial-failure match to the precedents is inferred from mechanism, not measured.
- No JWKS/secret-store or identity-provider component appears in reachable telemetry at any depth, and no component exposes a key-load/reload timestamp, so H1's per-pod stale-key mechanism cannot be directly observed.
- No control-plane pod-start/container-restart/config-reload/JWKS-reload event surface exists; the absence of a reload signal rests solely on the restarts counter being 0.
- Gateway keyword probes for 'clock' and 'jwks' were never executed (tool budget exhausted), so it cannot be fully ruled out that the gateway stream mentions JWKS reload timing or clock skew under wording other than 'kid'/'claim'/'signature' (which returned 0).
- kyc-provider has no telemetry and was not checked, so a silent failure or slowdown there cannot be excluded; this is untested rather than supported.
- Whether any other component shares the same rotated key or token-validation path was not queried, so a broader blast radius through a shared identity dependency remains unchecked.

## What would change this conclusion
- A rolling restart / forced config-and-JWKS reload of auth-service that shifts or clears the 401 rate would support H1 (stale kid on un-reloaded pods); a bit-for-bit invariant rate would support H2 (code-level token-shape rejection).
- Recoverable auth-service validation error text naming an unknown/mismatched kid would confirm H1; text naming a signature, clock-skew/expiry, or claim-parse failure would confirm H2.
