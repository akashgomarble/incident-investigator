# Incident investigation report: INC-012
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified (confidence 0.76, high): ledger-db's postgres data volume filled to 100% and now rejects every write, so payment-service — its loudest writer — reports "ledger write failed" at ~60% error rate. The fill was a pre-existing, monotonic climb (83.66% at 12:00Z → 100% by ~14:57–15:00Z) at a constant ~0.09 pct/min with no slope change at the 14:46Z v6.0.0 deploy, driven by the inactive replication slot 'analytics_cdc' steadily retaining tens of GB of WAL. Postgres PANICs on pg_wal/xlogtemp and base files with "No space left on device" from 15:01:01Z, and shared ledger-db users order-service and settlement-service degrade in the same window; payment-service's own resources and its bank-partner API are flat, and there were zero config changes. H2 (v6.0.0 mandate write pattern caused the fill) is disfavored because the deploy introduced no disk or WAL acceleration, and its decisive hop is falsified. The 15:12Z rollback changed nothing, consistent with a storage exhaustion that is not an application-state condition.

## Probable root cause
**ledger-db storage exhaustion (pre-existing disk fill, slot-retained WAL) makes all ledger-db writes fail; payment-service is the loudest victim** -- infrastructure_failure in `ledger-db`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.76, high)

### H1: ledger-db storage exhaustion (pre-existing disk fill, slot-retained WAL) makes all ledger-db writes fail; payment-service is the loudest victim
The shared ledger-db postgres data volume filled up over hours BEFORE the incident: 83.66% at 12:00Z, 90% by 13:10Z, 95% by 14:05Z, 97.3% at 14:30Z, 100% by ~14:57-15:00Z, at a constant ~0.09 pct/min with NO slope change at the 14:46Z deploy. A large, long-standing WAL backlog (inactive slot 'analytics_cdc' retaining 33.9-77.4 GB at a steady ~2400-3000 MB/min, unchanged across the deploy boundary) is the only quantitatively-reported space consumer in the window (F38). Once the volume is full, postgres cannot extend relation files or write pg_wal/xlogtemp, producing 'No space left on device' PANICs from 15:01:01Z and propagated 'ledger write failed' errors on payment-service from 15:00:26Z. Services actively using the ledger write path (payment, order, settlement) degrade from ~14:58Z; the app rollback at 15:12Z does not relieve the errors because storage exhaustion is not an application-state condition. This is a resource/infrastructure exhaustion whose onset is set by the disk-fill curve, not by dep-6203.

**Causal chain**
1. ledger-db's data volume sits on a monotonic growth curve starting hours before the incident: 83.66% (12:00Z) -> 90% (13:10Z) -> 95% (14:05Z) -> 97.48% (14:30Z) -> 98.4-98.8% (14:40-14:47Z) -> 100% (~14:57-15:00Z), constant ~0.09 pct/min, no step at 14:46Z. [F18, F19, F20, F35, F13]

11. portfolio-service (4th ledger-db user) shows no degradation: flat error_rate ~0.13-0.29, flat db_pool, one WARN line (a connection reset), zero 'ledger' log lines at INFO+; this is consistent with impact following services active on the ledger WRITE path, though no write-volume metric exists to confirm portfolio's write rate. ### H2: payment-service v6.0.0's mandate path introduced a write/entry-type pattern that is incompatible with ledger-db, and that pattern (not a pre-existing fill) is what pushed the volume to 100%
The new UPI mandate flow in dep-6203 writes a new MANDATE_HOLD ledger entry type; either it generates a write/temp-file volume surge, or it writes entries/relations that ledger-db cannot absorb or rejects, and that post-deploy behavior is the origin of the failure — with ledger-db disk exhaustion as its downstream symptom. The timing (deploy 14:46Z, errors 14:58-15:00Z) fits direct causation, and this hypothesis is NOT positively excluded because no write-volume metric, WAL-size metric, or schema/enum listing was obtainable.

2. The mandate flow produces a surge of ledger writes or entry-type/relation-specific writes that consume the remaining ~5% of the ledger-db volume within ~12 min, or that require a schema/enum change that accompanied the deploy. *(assumed)*
3. ledger-db hits 100% and cannot write pg_wal/xlogtemp or extend base files, producing 'No space left on device'. [F13, F9]
4. payment/order/settlement writes fail with 'No space left on device' from ~14:58Z, error rates 48-62%; payment-service logs 'ledger write failed'. [F7, F8, F11]
