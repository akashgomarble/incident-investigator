# Incident investigation report: INC-002
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified with medium confidence: config change cfg-2207, applied by infra-automation at 09:52Z, cut portfolio-service's Hikari datasource pool from 50 to 5 connections, exhausting the pool at normal concurrency (~12 concurrent connections) and stalling /v1/portfolio/*, which the api-gateway surfaced as upstream-timeout 504s. The reviewed analysis ranks H1 at 0.72 (locked, medium) and clearly ahead of all alternatives, each of which is positively contradicted: the gateway envoy/HTTP-2 upgrade (H2, dep-5220 at 08:00Z) precedes onset by ~1h51m and its expected HTTP/2 reset/protocol error signature is absent; the shared ledger-db stall (H3) is falsified by flat DB cpu/slow_queries, connections_active falling 62→55, and all three other ledger-db consumers flat; the redis branch of H3 is falsified by flat hit_ratio/evictions and flat redis consumers; and load-driven saturation (H4) is refuted by flat request rate ~566 req/s. The positive signature is portfolio-only and internal — Hikari "total=5, active=5, idle=0, waiting=43" from 09:53:19Z with db_pool_in_use pinned at exactly 5.0 — and cfg-2207 is the only change whose timestamp lands inside the 09:51–09:53Z onset window. Residual confidence limit: cfg-2207 is recorded at minute granularity with no accompanying restart/rollout event, so the exact activation second is unobservable and the tight 1-minute alignment with onset is correlation rather than a proven sequence.

## Probable root cause
**cfg-2207 cut portfolio-service datasource pool 50→5, exhausting Hikari and stalling /v1/portfolio/*** -- config_change in `portfolio-service`, triggered by `cfg-2207` (confidence 0.72, medium)

### H1: cfg-2207 cut portfolio-service datasource pool 50→5, exhausting Hikari and stalling /v1/portfolio/*
infra-automation applied cfg-2207 at 09:52Z, shrinking portfolio-service's Hikari datasource max pool from 50 to 5 ('cost-optimisation profile small'). At normal concurrency (~12 in-use connections pre-change) the 5-connection cap is insufficient; requests queue on the pool, exhaust the 3s acquisition timeout, and portfolio-service latency/error rate spike. The gateway converts the resulting slow upstream calls into 504s on /v1/portfolio/*. Ledger-db (flat cpu ~30%, slow_queries ~1/min, connections_active FALLING 62→55, all three other ledger-db consumers flat) and redis-cache (hit_ratio flat ~96%, evictions flat ~3/min, both redis consumers flat) are now both shown healthy at onset, leaving the 50→5 pool cut as the only change on the affected path. Residual confidence limit: the exact second the pool change became effective is not observable (minute-granularity, no restart), so the 1-minute alignment with onset is correlation rather than a proven sequence.

**Causal chain**
1. cfg-2207 applies db.pool.max_size 50->5 to portfolio-service at 09:52:00Z via infra-automation cost-optimisation profile; it is the only change on the portfolio path and the only change in the 09:00-10:30Z all-service sweep [F8, F13, F16, F17]
2. The new 5-connection cap is immediately exhausted: db_pool_in_use drops from ~12 and pins to exactly 5.0 from 09:55Z onward [F6]
