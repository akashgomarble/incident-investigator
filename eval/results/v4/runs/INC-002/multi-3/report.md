# Incident investigation report: INC-002
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified with high confidence (0.82): the portfolio-page timeouts and gateway p99/504 spikes were caused by config change cfg-2207, which shrank portfolio-service's db.pool.max_size from 50 to 5 at 09:52:00Z, saturating the connection pool and cascading upstream timeouts to api-gateway. The evidence chain is complete at every hop: a change inventory with exactly three items (F1/F13), the pool pinned flat at its new ceiling of 5.0 with flat CPU (F12), HikariPool total=5/active=5/idle=0/waiting=43 with "could not acquire JDBC connection within 3000ms" (F11), simultaneous portfolio-service and gateway latency/error steps (F7/F8/F10), and 252/255 gateway WARNs being upstream timeouts to portfolio-service (F9). The reporter's gateway HTTP/2 upgrade hypothesis (H2) is affirmatively contradicted on timing (1h52m quiet gap before onset), mechanism (an envoy/stream-layer fault cannot pin a Hikari pool at exactly 5), and scope (order-service, the other service that upgrade touched, stayed healthy). The gateway is a symptom carrier, not the origin. The one caveat keeping this inferential rather than confirmed is that neither suspect was ever reverted, so the decisive revert-and-recover test does not exist in the data.

## Probable root cause
**portfolio-service DB pool shrunk to 5 by cfg-2207, saturating the pool and cascading 504s to gateway** -- config_change in `portfolio-service`, triggered by `cfg-2207` (confidence 0.82, high)

### H1: portfolio-service DB pool shrunk to 5 by cfg-2207, saturating the pool and cascading 504s to gateway
Config change cfg-2207 on portfolio-service reduced db.pool.max_size from 50 to 5 at 09:52:00Z ('Apply cost-optimisation profile small to service datasource', author infra-automation). Normal portfolio-page concurrency needs more than 5 concurrent connections (baseline in-use ~11.4-13.3), so the pool pins at its ceiling (total=5, active=5, idle=0, waiters=43). Requests block ~3000ms trying to acquire a JDBC connection and then fail; portfolio-service latency/errors spike, api-gateway's upstream calls to portfolio-service time out (504), inflating gateway p99. The gateway is a symptom carrier, not the origin.

**Causal chain**
1. cfg-2207 lands on portfolio-service at 09:52:00Z reducing db.pool.max_size 50->5 [F1, F3, F13, F14]
2. Pool pins at its new ceiling 5: db_pool_in_use falls from ~12 to a flat 5.0 while cpu_pct stays flat ~35-36%, i.e. ceiling not load [F12]
3. Requests queue for JDBC connections; HikariPool reports total=5, active=5, idle=0, waiting=43 and 'could not acquire JDBC connection within 3000ms' from 09:53:19Z [F11]
4. portfolio-service p99 jumps from 176ms to ~1865-4546ms and error rate to ~22% in the 09:50-09:55 bucket [F10]
5. api-gateway upstream calls to portfolio-service time out: 252/255 gateway WARNs are 'upstream timeout portfolio-service GET /v1/portfolio/holdings -> 504' from 09:53:53Z, gateway p99 steps to ~1850-4400ms and error rate to ~7% [F9, F7, F8]
6. Fault is localized to the portfolio path: all other ledger-db consumers (order, payment, settlement) and the other HTTP/2-upgraded dependency (order-service) stay flat at 09:50-09:55Z, and ledger-db's own query/CPU load is flat [F24, F25, F26, F27, F28, F29]
