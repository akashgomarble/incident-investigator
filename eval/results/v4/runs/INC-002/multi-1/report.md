# Incident investigation report: INC-002
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified with high confidence (0.78): config change cfg-2207, applied by infra-automation at 2026-03-10T09:52Z, reduced portfolio-service db.pool.max_size from 50 to 5 via the "cost-optimisation profile small". Within ~4 minutes the HikariPool pegged at exactly total=5/active=5/idle=0 with waiting queues of 45-57 and 3000ms JDBC acquisition timeouts, portfolio-service began failing on /v1/portfolio/holdings, and the api-gateway — whose CPU stayed flat at ~40-46% — returned upstream-timeout 504s for that route. The suspected gateway envoy 1.29->1.30 / HTTP-2 upgrade (dep-5220, 08:00Z) precedes onset by ~2h with no intervening event and its known precedent (HIST-116) presents as "upstream resets", a signature absent from today's logs. The fix is to revert/raise db.pool.max_size; all changes remain live as of 11:59Z, so the incident is likely ongoing.

## Probable root cause

### H1: portfolio-service HikariPool shrunk to 5 by cost-optimisation config, pool pegs and 504s propagate to gateway
On 2026-03-10T09:52Z infra-automation applied cfg-2207, reducing portfolio-service db.pool.max_size from 50 to 5 ('cost-optimisation profile small'). Under normal concurrent holdings traffic the 5-slot HikariPool saturates within ~1 minute; requests block for the 3000ms acquisition timeout, the pool pins at exactly 5 in-use (idle=0, waiting up to 57), and portfolio-service returns errors/slow responses. The api-gateway's upstream calls to portfolio-service then exceed their timeout, producing the 504 'upstream timeout portfolio-service GET /v1/portfolio/holdings' pattern and the gateway p99 spike. The gateway, and everything else upstream, is a victim.

**Causal chain**
1. cfg-2207 applied at 09:52Z reduces portfolio-service Datasource pool max_size 50 -> 5 [F2, F13]
2. Within ~1-4 minutes the 5-slot pool is fully occupied and new requests cannot acquire a JDBC connection (3000ms acquisition timeout; total=5, active=5, idle=0, waiting 45-57) [F9, F10]
3. In-use connections pin at exactly 5.0 from the 09:56Z bucket onward, replacing a ~12-connection baseline, i.e. the cap is pegged [F11]
4. portfolio-service latency p99 jumps (09:45Z bucket avg ~2080ms / max 4546ms) and errors begin at 09:53:19Z [F9]
5. api-gateway calls to portfolio-service wait on the saturated upstream and time out: 252 WARNs of exactly 'upstream timeout portfolio-service GET /v1/portfolio/holdings', first at 09:53:53Z; gateway p99 200ms -> ~1.8-4.3s from the 09:50Z bucket [F8, F7]
6. Users see the portfolio page spinning / timing out (SEV-2 report at 10:05Z) *(assumed)*

The pool saturation observed in portfolio-service would then be a symptom of the shared-store fault, not of the pool-size config; the gateway is two hops removed.
