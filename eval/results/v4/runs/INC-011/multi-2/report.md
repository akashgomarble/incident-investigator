# Incident investigation report: INC-011
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified (confidence 0.80, high): the pricing-engine config flag flags.pricing.greeks_v2.rollout_pct was ramped 5→100% at 12:04:00Z, moving essentially all quote traffic onto the CPU-bound GreeksV2 Monte-Carlo path (paths=20000), which saturates pricing-engine CPU and blows the 2000ms compute budget; order-service, calling pricing-engine synchronously with a 2500ms timeout, then fails at the quote step. Temporal and causal fit are tight — pricing-engine p99 steps 58.67ms→2528.15ms between 12:03Z and 12:05Z, CPU steps in exactly the 12:04Z bucket matching the config timestamp, and order-service quote timeouts begin 12:06:10Z, ~1 min later — while the compute-bound log signatures name the GreeksV2 calculator and the 2000ms deadline. Competing alternatives are positively contradicted: load growth is excluded by dead-flat request volume, the market-data-service protobuf deploy shows no latency/error discontinuity, and redis-cache health metrics are flat. The one unverified hop is the path-mix shift itself (no per-calculator counter exists), which is why confidence is capped at 0.80 rather than higher.
