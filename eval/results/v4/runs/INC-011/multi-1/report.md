# Incident investigation report: INC-011
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified with medium confidence (0.72): the pricing-engine config ramp cfg-3120 (flags.pricing.greeks_v2.rollout_pct 5→100 at 12:04Z) turned on the expensive GreeksV2 Monte-Carlo calculator (20000 paths, 6.5–8.4s per call) for all traffic, pinning pricing-engine CPU at ~99% and driving p99 from ~60ms to ~2535ms — far beyond its own 2000ms quote budget — so order-service's 2500ms client calls timed out. The onset is strictly one minute after the ramp (CPU 38–42% through 12:04Z → 98.65% at 12:05Z; p99 56.8–63.1ms → 2534.9ms) with zero precursor, and capacity loss is excluded (flat request_rate, 0 restarts, flat memory). The competing hypotheses are contradicted by direct telemetry: market-data-service's 11:56Z protobuf deploy shows flat p99/error_rate and pricing-engine was unaffected for 8+ minutes after it, and redis-cache metrics are flat. No revert of the flag has occurred, so the decisive recovery-on-revert confirmation is absent, but the flag ramp is the only change in the window and the causal chain is temporally tight.

## Probable root cause

### H1: Greeks v2 flag ramp to 100% saturates pricing-engine CPU → quote budget exceeded → order-service timeouts
A config change on pricing-engine (flags.pricing.greeks_v2.rollout_pct 5→100 at 12:04Z, cfg-3120) turned on the expensive GreeksV2 Monte-Carlo calculator for essentially all traffic. The 20000-path Monte-Carlo computation (6.5-8.4s per call) pinned pricing-engine CPU at ~99% and drove p99 from ~60ms to ~2528ms, so pricing-engine blew its own 2000ms quote budget and returned too slowly for order-service's 2500ms client timeout, producing the reported quote timeouts. The new 1-minute-resolution data (F25-F30) resolves the critic's central objection: the onset is strictly at 12:05Z with zero precursor, exactly one minute after the flag ramp, while capacity proxies (request_rate, restarts, memory) are unchanged.

**Causal chain**

2. GreeksV2 Monte-Carlo (paths=20000) becomes active for ~all traffic at the ramp; first WARN at 12:05:21Z, zero WARNs anywhere in 11:50-12:05Z [F8, F23, F27]
3. pricing-engine CPU steps from a flat 38-42% baseline (every 1-min bucket 11:50-12:04Z) to 98.65% at 12:05Z and 99.25-100% thereafter [F11, F25]
