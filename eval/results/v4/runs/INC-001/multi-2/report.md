# Incident investigation report: INC-001
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Order placement 500s are caused by the order-service deployment dep-5101 (v2.14.0, 12:10Z), whose new MarginCheckValidator dereferences a null margin profile on the F&O path and throws NullPointerException at MarginCheckValidator.java:88; confidence is high (0.80, capped by the Critic). The chain is: the only change anywhere in the window is dep-5101 into exactly the class/file where the exception fires; the 500 rate steps from ~0.2% to ~18% at 12:12Z, immediately after the deploy; the failure is an exhaustive, deterministic in-process NPE (426/426 ERROR lines, two patterns, no third class, none before 12:12:31Z); it is confined to orderType=FNO, matching the newly added F&O margin check; and order-service resources and all six reachable neighbours (including the shared ledger-db consumers) are flat at ~0.17-0.26% with no step at onset. Competing origins are contradicted by evidence: no config/flag change touched any service's margin/FNO path, no resource saturation or timeout signature exists, and auth-service had zero changes. The residual uncertainty is that the null marginProfile's ultimate supply origin is established only by the absence of any modelled supplier; the fix (rollback plus null-tolerant margin handling) is the same either way.

## Probable root cause
**order-service v2.14.0 (dep-5101) ships a margin check that dereferences a null margin profile on the F&O path — local NPE** -- bad_deployment in `order-service`, triggered by `dep-5101` (confidence 0.80, high)

### H1: order-service v2.14.0 (dep-5101) ships a margin check that dereferences a null margin profile on the F&O path — local NPE
Deployment dep-5101 (order-service v2.14.0, 12:10Z, 'Refactor order validation; add margin check for F&O orders') replaced the legacy OrderRules.validateMargin() with a new MarginCheckValidator in the validation chain. The new validator dereferences a margin profile that is absent/null for F&O accounts, throwing NullPointerException at MarginCheckValidator.java:88 ('marginProfile is null for account <id>') and returning HTTP 500 on order placement. Origin is the order-service code change itself. The FNO skew is not a separate phenomenon: only orderType=FNO orders route through the newly added F&O margin check, and the error text never even contains the word 'derivatives' — FNO is the only machine-readable classification present.
