# Incident investigation report: INC-006
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The quote-latency degradation in INC-006 is best explained by H1 (confidence 0.74, medium): loss of the shared redis-cache primary followed by a failover that promoted an EMPTY-dataset replica, producing a cache-miss storm on market-data-service and cascading deadline-exceeded failures into pricing-engine. The chain is temporally tight and correctly ordered at 1-minute resolution: redis-cache-2 marked down 13:22:10Z (first cache WARN/ERROR, F15/F31), hit_ratio cliff from ~96% to 10.45% at 13:22Z (F14), empty-dataset failover at 13:22:52Z (F10), market-data-service miss storm at 13:22:25Z (F11) with its p99 stepping 40ms→1850ms and error_rate stepping at 13:22Z (F33/F34) one full minute before pricing-engine's error step at 13:23Z and first deadline ERROR at 13:23:57Z naming market-data-service (F34/F35), while pricing-engine's own CPU/mem stayed flat (F13). H2 is now closed for every service in pricing-engine's dependency closure (all partial:false, count 0: F20–F24, F26) and survives only as an unevidenced host/infra-layer variant; H3 and H4 are correctly rejected. The proximate trigger of the redis-cache-2 silence remains unexplained — no host/node telemetry exists (F32) and no OOM/maxmemory/shutdown line precedes it (F31) — but the discriminating evidence (flat-then-cliff memory 74.63%→6.0% and client loss ~87% in one minute, no eviction burst, flat CPU) points to an abrupt process/node loss rather than resource exhaustion, so the fix (restore and re-warm the shared cache, harden empty-dataset failover) is well defined.

### H1: Redis-cache primary loss with empty-dataset failover → cache miss storm → downstream quote latency
The shared redis-cache primary (redis-cache-2) went silent on health checks and was marked down at 13:22:10Z, and the failover promoted replica redis-cache-3 with an EMPTY dataset at 13:22:52Z. With no cache contents, market-data-service's quote lookups all missed and had to rebuild from the exchange feed snapshot (289-occurrence cache miss storm, first_seen 13:22:25Z), which inflated its p99 from ~40ms to ~1850-1980ms and its error rate from ~0.2% to ~3.2% at 13:22Z. pricing-engine, which calls market-data-service over HTTP and whose own CPU/mem stayed flat (38-41% / 47-51%), then exceeded its quote-computation deadline one minute later (error rate steps at 13:23Z, first deadline ERROR 13:23:57Z) and surfaced 2-3s quote loads to trading screens. The redis failure is the FIRST event in the sequence; everything downstream follows it in strict order.
