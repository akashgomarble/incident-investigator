# Incident investigation report: INC-004
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The three OOMKilled restarts of notification-service are best explained by H1 (confidence 0.72, medium): the unbounded in-memory TemplateCache introduced by deployment dep-5402 (notification-service v3.8.0, "Add in-memory template cache for email rendering") grows linearly and without eviction until the container hits its memory limit. The evidence is temporally ordered and consistent: memory_pct is flat through 2026-03-21T01:00Z and then climbs monotonically starting immediately after the only relevant deployment [F9, F26], TemplateCache entry counts rise strictly linearly with evictions=0 and reset exactly at each restart [F28], and the three restarts form a clean sawtooth at ~46-47% floor and ~94-98% peak [F7, F8, F10, F27, F30]. The reporter's Kafka-replay hypothesis (H2, 0.04) is contradicted on three axes — two of three OOMs predate cfg-2410, notification-service lag is flat inside the cfg-2410 window and spikes only at/after its own restart, and cfg-2410 changed exactly one retention key with no consumer-group/partition impact [F18, F22, F23, F35]. H3 (0.03) and H4 (0.04) have only negative or absent support [F34, F21, F33]. The conclusion stops short of high confidence because the unboundedness of the cache is inferred from the absence of an eviction policy in a two-line change record rather than from code, and memory_pct plus cache-entry logs are the only memory signals available for this service [F25, F31, F32].

## Probable root cause
**Unbounded in-memory TemplateCache from dep-5402 leaks memory until OOM** -- bad_deployment in `notification-service`, triggered by `dep-5402` (confidence 0.72, medium)

### H1: Unbounded in-memory TemplateCache from dep-5402 leaks memory until OOM
notification-service v3.8.0 (dep-5402) introduced an in-memory ConcurrentHashMap TemplateCache keyed by template+locale+userSegment on the render path. The cache has no eviction/TTL/max-size policy, so its entry count grows without bound at a near-constant rate as new key combinations are seen across all traffic; heap grows steadily until the container reaches its memory limit and is OOMKilled. Each restart clears the heap, then the cache refills along the same trajectory, producing the observed sawtooth with ~46-50% residual after each restart.

**Causal chain**
1. dep-5402 deploys notification-service v3.8.0 adding a ConcurrentHashMap TemplateCache keyed by template+locale+userSegment to the email render path, and remains live with no rollback [F1, F2]
2. The cache has no eviction/TTL/max-size policy in the retrieved change record and its key space is bounded only by (template x locale x userSegment) cardinality, which nothing caps [F31, F32]
3. TemplateCache stats logs show strictly linear, unbounded entry growth with evictions=0, resetting exactly at each restart, with near-identical trajectories across cycles [F28]
4. memory_pct, flat at ~47-49% through 03-21T00:00Z, begins a steady monotonic climb at 03-21T01:00-02:00Z, hours before any other change and consistent with an ever-growing cache rather than a queue-driven burst [F9, F26]
5. Heap reaches ~94-97% and the pod is OOMKilled (exit 137); the restart clears the heap to ~46%, then it climbs again along the same slope, giving a sawtooth across all three restarts on 03-21, 03-22 and 03-23 [F7, F8, F10, F27, F30]
6. Growth is independent of traffic: request_rate is flat across all four days with no step at the climb onset, and cpu_pct is dead flat at ~25%, so the growth is not workload-volume driven [F29]
7. Consumer lag stays normal until each restart and only spikes in the same bucket as (or after) each OOM, so the lag spike is a consequence of the restart/consumer stall rather than a precursor of the memory climb [F11, F18, F22, F23]
8. The service is unavailable during each restart, delaying trade-confirmation emails ~25 min [F7]
9. No other deployment or config change exists in the window that could explain the memory trend (no notification-service config/limit change, and no deployment at all on 03-23) [F33, F34]

### H3: Node/container-level resource constraint: lowered memory limit or noisy neighbour
The OOMKills stem from a container memory-limit reduction or node-level memory contention on the host running notification-service, not from service code. The pod is killed by the kernel/cgroup despite only moderate apparent application growth.
