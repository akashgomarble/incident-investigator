# Incident investigation report: INC-004
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The three OOMKills of notification-service are caused by the in-heap TemplateCache added by deployment dep-5402 (v3.8.0) on 2026-03-21T00:00Z, which grows without eviction and steadily consumes heap until the container limit is hit (confidence 0.87, high). The evidence chain is tight: memory was flat ~47.7-48.5% all of 03-20, began ramping within ~1.7h of the deploy, and since then has formed a near-identical saw-tooth at a constant ~2%/hour slope that resets only at each restart, with flat request_rate and zero evictions while cache entries rise monotonically. The reporter's Kafka-replay theory is directly contradicted — the two earlier restarts predate the 18:00Z cfg-2410 retention change by 1-2 days, there is no 18:00Z inflection in memory, broker ingest/replication metrics are flat, and the co-tenant shared-Kafka consumer settlement-service shows no anomaly at all. Consumer lag and delayed emails are downstream symptoms of the restart, not the cause. Residual uncertainty (cache unboundedness inferred, not read from code; email-delay attribution assumed) caps confidence below 0.9 but does not support any competing origin.

## Probable root cause
**In-heap TemplateCache introduced by dep-5402 v3.8.0 grows unbounded, exhausting heap and causing OOMKills** -- bad_deployment in `notification-service`, triggered by `dep-5402` (confidence 0.87, high)

### H1: In-heap TemplateCache introduced by dep-5402 v3.8.0 grows unbounded, exhausting heap and causing OOMKills
The 2026-03-21 deployment dep-5402 (v3.8.0) added an in-memory ConcurrentHashMap template cache keyed by template+locale+userSegment with no eviction. Heap fills steadily at a constant rate as distinct cache keys accumulate, old-gen pressure triggers full GCs, and the container hits its memory limit and is OOMKilled. Three near-identical saw-tooth cycles follow the deploy. Consumer lag and delayed emails are downstream symptoms of the restart, not the cause.

**Causal chain**

2. The memory ramp onsets within the hour after the 2026-03-21T00:00Z deploy: memory_pct is flat at ~47.7-48.5% for all of pre-deploy day 03-20, then climbs in the 03-21T01:40Z bucket (51.77%). [F31]
