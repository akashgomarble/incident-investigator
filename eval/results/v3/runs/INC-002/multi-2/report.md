# Incident investigation report: INC-002
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The root cause of INC-002 is cfg-2207, the infra-automation cost-optimisation profile applied to portfolio-service at 2026-03-10T09:52:00Z, which cut db.pool.max_size from 50 to 5 and exhausted the JDBC connection pool on the portfolio holdings path (confidence 0.80, high; top hypothesis clearly ahead of the rejected alternatives). At 60s resolution both api-gateway and portfolio-service were flat-healthy through the 09:52Z minute and stepped together in the 09:53Z minute, so onset is strictly after the cap change; portfolio-service then logged HikariPool-1 exhaustion (total=5, active=5, idle=0, waiting=43) and JDBC acquire timeouts, causing 503s upstream and gateway 504 'upstream timeout portfolio-service' on /v1/portfolio/holdings. Traffic (526-596 req/s) and portfolio-service CPU/memory were flat, so the new cap — not load or resource starvation — is the binding constraint. The reporter's suspicion, the 08:00Z gateway HTTP/2 upgrade dep-5220, is undermined: it precedes onset by 108 minutes with no intermediate drift, shows a timeout-not-reset signature, and cannot produce an upstream JDBC-acquire error.

## Probable root cause

### H1: cfg-2207 shrank the portfolio-service JDBC pool 50→5, exhausting connections and stalling the portfolio path
At 09:52Z infra-automation applied cost-optimisation profile 'small' to portfolio-service's datasource, cutting db.pool.max_size from 50 to 5 (cfg-2207). The cap took effect at ~09:52-09:54Z (db_pool_in_use locks at exactly 5.0 from 09:54Z). Concurrent holdings queries then had to share 5 connections; the waiting queue reached 43 and requests failed to acquire a JDBC connection within 3000ms. portfolio-service fell over to 503s and its p99 jumped from ~173ms to ~4.25s in the same 09:53Z minute. The gateway, routing /v1/portfolio/* to portfolio-service, blocked on that upstream and returned 504 'upstream timeout portfolio-service' from 09:53:53Z. Both the gateway and upstream latency/error steps now resolve to the 09:53-09:54Z minute, i.e. strictly AFTER cfg-2207 applied at 09:52Z, and both series were flat-healthy through 09:52Z inclusive.

### H2: Gateway HTTP/2-to-upstreams upgrade (dep-5220) introduced a connection/stream-handling regression
dep-5220 (api-gateway v9.3.0, 08:00Z) switched envoy 1.29->1.30 and enabled HTTP/2 to portfolio-service and order-service. The upgraded filter chain mishandles upstream H2 connections/streams, so requests to portfolio-service hang until the upstream timeout, yielding p99~4.4s and 504s on /v1/portfolio/*. This is the reporter's suspicion and mirrors HIST-116.

**Causal chain**
1. dep-5220 deployed 08:00Z: envoy 1.29->1.30, upstream protocol h2 for portfolio-service and order-service [F1, F13]
2. New envoy/H2 stream handling limits concurrency or resets streams to portfolio-service *(assumed)*
3. Requests stall until the gateway upstream timeout and the gateway emits 504s on /v1/portfolio/* [F9]
4. Users see the portfolio page spin and time out [F7, F8]
