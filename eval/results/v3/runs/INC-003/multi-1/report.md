# Incident investigation report: INC-003
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The incident is caused by degradation of the external exchange-api (vendor) behind exchange-gateway: exchange-gateway's upstream_error_rate/upstream_latency_ms rise in the same 10:59Z bucket as its own error_rate/latency_p99 and plateau in lockstep to 12:59Z, with measured upstream latency (~9,000-10,000ms) exceeding exchange-gateway's own capped p99 (~5,000-5,400ms) — i.e. the ~5s stall is a client-side abort of a longer stall that lives beyond exchange-gateway (H1, confidence 0.72, medium). order-service then blocks synchronously on that call during placeOrder, producing ~30% "exchange-gateway call failed: 504 Gateway Timeout (placeOrder)" errors. The order-service v2.15.2 release is not the cause: onset was ~12 min after rollout completion, errors persisted at ~30% after rollback completed, the error signature is an upstream 504 (not a handler exception), and order-service resource metrics are flat. The rollback already performed cannot resolve this; escalation/failover to the vendor route is required.

## Probable root cause
**External exchange-api degradation (vendor side) stalls exchange-gateway, surfacing as 504s on order submit** -- dependency_failure in `exchange-api (external vendor)`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.72, medium)

### H1: External exchange-api degradation (vendor side) stalls exchange-gateway, surfacing as 504s on order submit
The external exchange-api (vendor, has_telemetry=false) degraded around 10:59Z. exchange-gateway, whose only downstream dependency is exchange-api, saw its calls to it stall ~9.0-10.0s, driving exchange-gateway error_rate to ~43-47% and latency_p99 to ~5.0-5.4s (its client-side cutoff). order-service, which synchronously calls exchange-gateway during placeOrder, blocked on those ~5s timeouts, producing 504 'exchange-gateway call failed' errors at ~30% and latency_p99 ~5.2s. No platform-side deploy or config change touched exchange-gateway or any of its in-platform dependencies, so the origin is outside the platform.

**Causal chain**
1. External exchange-api degrades (partner-side outage/latency); no in-window platform change touched exchange-gateway or any of its in-platform deps [F15, F16, F11, F9, F10]
2. exchange-gateway's own error_rate and latency_p99 step in the same 10:59Z bucket as its upstream_error_rate/upstream_latency_ms, with missing_points=0 — i.e. the self-failure is bound to the upstream call, not to a separate internal onset [F19, F22, F23]
3. The measured upstream latency (~9,000-10,000ms) exceeds exchange-gateway's own p99 (~5,000-5,400ms) in the same buckets, so the ~5s self-latency is the cutoff applied to a longer external stall — the delay originates at the external call, not inside exchange-gateway [F20]
4. exchange-gateway is not internally saturated: cpu_pct flat 26.7-32.5%, memory_pct flat 39.4-48.0%, request_rate flat ~245-298 req/s (no load spike), restarts=0 [F21, F24, F25, F26, F27]
