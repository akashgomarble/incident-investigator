*System: multi_agent | outcome: **inconclusive** | ended: max_rounds_reached*

## Summary
INC-007 is INCONCLUSIVE: the origin is bracketed to auth-service with near-certainty, but the evidence cannot separate the two candidate causes. Exactly two changes exist on the auth-service token-validation path across a 38-hour window — dep-5710 (auth-service v7.3.0, jjwt 0.11->0.12 "breaking parser API" + TokenValidator rewrite, deployed 14:00:00Z) and cfg-2705 (jwt.signing.key_id k-2025-10 -> k-2026-04, 14:01:00Z) — and the failure is a clean step from ~0.2% to ~36% localized to between 13:59Z and 14:01Z, with flat CPU/memory/latency/restarts and unchanged request rate (F2/F3/F12/F13/F6/F7/F8/F10/F11/F23). H1 (key rotation) and H2 (JWT library/TokenValidator change) stand at equal low confidence (0.30 each): they are one minute apart, both live and un-reverted, each resting on exactly one load-bearing unevidenced hop. Every analytic discriminator is exhausted — auth-service logs are unsearchable (identity-namespace log pipeline down), no kid/issuer/validation-outcome or per-pod metric dimension exists anywhere on the identity path, and no dependency timeseries was retrievable. H3 (shared dependency) is unresolved rather than dismissed, because the blast-radius series for the other three redis-cache consumers was never obtainable; H4/H5 are properly contradicted. The decisive next evidence is behavioral and operational: revert one change and observe the error-rate recovery, then the other — plus the shared-cache consumer series.

### H1: Scheduled signing-key rotation (cfg-2705) — replacement of jwt.signing.key_id rejects old-key-id tokens

If the flip was a REPLACEMENT rather than a dual-key overlap — i.e. the old key id is no longer accepted and no cached/stale key material retains it — then tokens signed/validated under k-2025-10 fail signature verification and surface as 401 invalid_token on a roughly constant fraction of traffic. The mechanism (replacement vs overlap; in-process vs shared key source; which subset of tokens/pods is stale) is UNRESOLVED: F17/F37 show only a single scalar old->new value with no companion 'retain previous key' entry, and F38/F42 note absence in a change log is not proof the old key was dropped. Per the critic, the load-bearing hop (old-kid tokens are actually rejected) is ASSUMED with zero direct evidence, so this cannot be ranked above H2.
