# Incident investigation report: INC-011
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The evidence supports H1 (confidence 0.72, medium): the pricing-engine config change cfg-3120, which ramped flags.pricing.greeks_v2.rollout_pct from 5 to 100 at 2026-05-20T12:04:00Z, moved quote traffic onto the compute-bound GreeksV2 Monte-Carlo calculator path, saturating pricing-engine CPU at ~99% and pushing its p99 from 57.5ms to ~2.6-2.85s, which blew order-service's 2500ms synchronous quote budget and produced the timeouts. The onset is a sharp multi-signal step landing on the exact minute of the flag ramp (latency, CPU, error_rate, first GreeksV2 WARN at 12:05:21Z), while request_rate and memory are flat, ruling out load- and GC-driven saturation; the blast radius is confined to the pricing-engine -> order-service quote path. The reporter's market-data-service protobuf suspicion (H2) is refuted on timing (11:56 deploy produced no metric step anywhere, 8-minute silent gap) and mechanism (compute path, not parse path), with the service itself and its other consumer exchange-gateway flat and silent. The key residual limitation is that no per-request/per-calculator/metric-level flag-effect trace exists, so the causal link rests on the minute-level coincidence of cfg-3120 with the step plus generic calculator logs — this is a permanent observability limit, not evidence for an alternative cause, and it is why confidence is medium rather than high.

## Probable root cause
**Greeks v2 feature-flag ramp to 100% on pricing-engine overloads the Monte-Carlo calculator path** -- config_change in `pricing-engine`, triggered by `cfg-3120` (confidence 0.72, medium)

### H1: Greeks v2 feature-flag ramp to 100% on pricing-engine overloads the Monte-Carlo calculator path
At 2026-05-20T12:04:00Z the pricing-engine config change cfg-3120 ramped flags.pricing.greeks_v2.rollout_pct from 5 to 100, moving essentially all quote requests onto the compute-bound GreeksV2 Monte-Carlo calculator path. That path is expensive (20000 paths, 2964-8942ms per symbol), which pinned pricing-engine CPU at ~99% (matching HIST-113's signature) and blew its 2000ms internal compute budget, raising pricing-engine p99 from 57.5ms to ~2.6-2.85s. order-service, which calls pricing-engine synchronously with a 2500ms budget, then timed out on the quote step.

2. The suddenly-dominant calculator is compute-bound Monte-Carlo work: 'GreeksV2Calculator: Monte-Carlo paths=20000 took <2964-8942>ms' WARNs begin at 12:05:21Z, and no GreeksV2 WARN exists before the 12:04:16 config line. [F12, F17, F35]
