# Incident investigation report: INC-012
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The incident is a shared ledger-db disk-exhaustion event, not a payment-service v6.0.0 defect: the DB's /var/lib/postgresql/data filled monotonically to exactly 100% at ~14:56-14:58Z, after which every ledger write failed with "could not extend file: No space left on device". The top hypothesis H2 (retained WAL from the inactive replication slot analytics_cdc exhausting the disk) is ranked first at confidence 0.72 (medium) and is clearly ahead of all rejected alternatives (H4/H5/H1/H3 at 0.02-0.05). Confidence is capped at medium because WAL retention is inferred from metric shape (no WAL-retention gauge exists) and the ledger-write failure signature is log-evidenced for only two of the four ledger writers. The decisive discriminator is that the 15:12Z v6.0.0 rollback changed nothing: error rates for payment/order/settlement stayed flat at ~60/47/55% and disk stayed pinned at 100.0% through the end of data at 16:58Z, ruling out any v6.0.0-code contribution. Recovery was never observed within the window; the incident was still fully impaired at 16:59Z.

## Probable root cause
**ledger-db disk exhaustion from retained WAL forces all ledger writes to fail** -- resource_exhaustion in `ledger-db`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.72, medium)

### H2: ledger-db disk exhaustion from retained WAL forces all ledger writes to fail

The failure is independent of v6.0.0: it persists unchanged after the 15:12Z rollback and stays elevated through 16:58Z while disk remains pinned at 100%.
