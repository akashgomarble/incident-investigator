# Incident investigation report: INC-003
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The ~30% order-submit failure rate is a downstream symptom of the external exchange partner (exchange-api) failing behind exchange-gateway: exchange-gateway's own upstream error rate stepped 0.30%→40.87% and upstream latency 120ms→~9400ms at 10:58–11:00Z, before order-service's first 504 at 11:02:45Z (F14, F10, F15). exchange-gateway's logs show 5000ms connect timeouts to api.exchange-partner.example plus partner-generated HTTP 503s carrying the partner's own x-partner-request-id, proving the partner is reachable but slow/failing rather than a network/mesh break (F24, F25, F26, F27). order-service v2.15.2 (dep-5333) is coincidental: its 11:16Z rollback changed nothing (errors flat ~30-33%, p99 ~5.0-5.7s through 12:58Z), and the deploy minute itself was normal (F13, F15, G/F8). Confidence is high (0.78 per reviewed ranking); the residual uncertainty is only whether the partner's fault is partner-side overload vs partner-side network, since exchange-api has no telemetry (F19, F20).

## Probable root cause
**exchange-gateway's upstream dependency (external exchange partner, exchange-api) is failing — 504s cascade to order-service** -- dependency_failure in `exchange-api (external exchange partner, upstream of exchange-gateway)`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.78, high)
