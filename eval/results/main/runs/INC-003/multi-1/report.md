# Incident investigation report: INC-003
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The order-submit failures are caused by degradation of the external exchange partner (api.exchange-partner.example) reached via exchange-gateway, not by order-service v2.15.2 — confidence high (top hypothesis H1 at 0.82, clearly ahead of H2 at 0.08 and all others rejected). The causal chain is verified end-to-end: exchange-gateway's first upstream error (upstream connect timeout after 5000ms, host=api.exchange-partner.example) appears at 11:01:25Z, ~80s BEFORE order-service's first error at 11:02:45Z; 100% of order-service's 349 error lines are 'exchange-gateway call failed: 504 Gateway Timeout (placeOrder)'; exchange-gateway's upstream latency to the partner steps from ~119ms to ~9.2s while its own CPU/memory/restarts and connection pool are flat/healthy and its inbound volume is unchanged. Order-service's own resources are flat (CPU ~39-41%, pool ~14, 0 restarts), and the 11:16Z rollback changed nothing — the error rate is a flat ~30-31% step from 11:02Z through 12:55Z across the rollback boundary. The same-timed 11:00Z error step in market-data-service and api-gateway (independent paths) further indicates a partner-wide external event rather than an order-service defect, so the rollback in flight will not fix the incident.

## Probable root cause
**External exchange partner (api.exchange-partner.example) degraded — order-service surfaces 504s from exchange-gateway** -- dependency_failure in `api.exchange-partner.example (external exchange partner, reached via exchange-gateway)`, triggered by `null` (confidence 0.82, high)

### H1: External exchange partner (api.exchange-partner.example) degraded — order-service surfaces 504s from exchange-gateway
The external exchange partner reached via exchange-gateway began timing out/returning 503 around 11:01Z. exchange-gateway's call quality to the partner stepped sharply (upstream_error_rate 0.2%->45%, upstream_latency 119ms->~9.2s) with NO change in call volume (flat ~272 req/s) and NO local resource/connection-stress signature (CPU flat 27-31%, memory 42-47%, pool 'healthy', 0 restarts). order-service's placeOrder fan-out to exchange-gateway then blocked on the 5000ms timeout, producing the ~5.2s p99 plateau and the 30% 'exchange-gateway call failed: 504 Gateway Timeout (placeOrder)' error rate. Independent services on different paths (market-data-service, api-gateway) show the same-timed step with no local fault, consistent with a partner-wide/external event. Rollback of order-service cannot fix this.

**Causal chain**
1. External exchange partner degrades: exchange-gateway's calls to host=api.exchange-partner.example begin connect-timeout'ing after 5000ms and returning 503 at 11:01:25Z, the earliest failing telemetry in the window (before order-service's first error by ~80s) [F10, F16, F24]
2. This is a QUALITY change not a volume/resource change: exchange-gateway's inbound request_rate is flat (~250-292 req/s across 10:45-11:02, mean 272), its CPU 27-31%, memory 42-47%, restarts 0, and its own logs report 'connection pool stats: healthy' (10:58:27Z) and 'health check ok' (11:02:01Z); its upstream_error_rate steps 0.3%->45%, upstream_latency 119ms->8248/9200ms [F22, F20, F21, F23, F18]
3. The failure is confined to the gateway->partner hop: all exchange-gateway ERROR lines are partner-facing timeouts/503s (none local pool/executor), and its circuit breaker opens at 11:03:26Z [F21, F25, F27]
4.
