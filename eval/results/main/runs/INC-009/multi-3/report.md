# Incident investigation report: INC-009
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
The most likely root cause of INC-009 is the portfolio-service deployment dep-5905 (v4.5.0, 2026-05-05T08:56:00Z), which shipped a new unindexed query `SELECT * FROM trades WHERE account_id=$1 AND executed_at > $2 ORDER BY executed_at`; it drives Seq Scans over ~48M rows on the shared ledger-db, saturating DB CPU/connections and starving co-located callers — including order-service, whose only error signature is INSERT INTO orders statement timeouts. Confidence is high (0.86, Critic-accepted). At 60s resolution every monitored signal is flat through the 08:57 bucket and jumps in the 08:58:00Z bucket, 2 minutes after the deploy and 58 minutes after the unrelated 08:00Z DBA autovacuum change cfg-2903 (which produced no measurable DB load response for ~50 minutes). The DB-side mechanism is statement-level verified: all 360 ledger-db WARN slow-query entries in 08:58–09:04Z are that exact predicate under application_name=portfolio-service with Seq Scan rows=48211907, first seen 08:58:38Z; the aggressor (portfolio-service) degrades in the same 08:58 bucket as ledger-db while the victim (order-service) lags one bucket at 08:59. Alternative hypotheses (pure autovacuum causality, generic shared-DB capacity collapse, external IOPS throttling) are contradicted by the absence of any pre-08:58 load response, the 2-of-4 selective impact pattern (payment-service and settlement-service are flat), and the absence of any IOPS telemetry or supporting precedent.

## Probable root cause
**portfolio-service deploy dep-5905 introduced an unindexed trades scan that saturated shared ledger-db (fault originates in bad_deployment)** -- bad_deployment in `portfolio-service`, triggered by `dep-5905` (confidence 0.86, high)
