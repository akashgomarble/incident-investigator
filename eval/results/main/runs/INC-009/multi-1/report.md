# Incident investigation report: INC-009
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
Root cause identified with high confidence (0.88): portfolio-service v4.5.0 (dep-5905), rolled out at 08:56:32Z, began issuing a new unindexed reporting query against the shared ledger-db table 'trades' (SELECT * FROM trades WHERE account_id=$1 AND executed_at > $2 ORDER BY executed_at), which executes as a Seq Scan over ~48M rows. That query load drove ledger-db CPU to ~95-96%, connections_active from ~60 to ~190, and slow_queries from ~1/min to ~139/min within a single minute (08:57Z -> 08:58Z), starving order-service's INSERT INTO orders writes, which then hit ledger-db statement timeouts and drove order-service p99 from ~150ms to ~5300ms. order-service itself was never changed (no deploy in the window), and the reporter's autovacuum suspicion (H2) is contradicted by timing (~48 min lead with no intermediate degradation), signature (no IO/disk/bloat pressure; disk flat at ~71%), and absence of precedent. H3 (infra/IOPS/failover) was actively tested and contradicted: no host/volume/failover event exists in any feed for 2026-05-05, connections rose rather than collapsed, and disk stayed flat.

## Probable root cause
**New portfolio-service unindexed trades query saturates shared ledger-db, starving order-service** -- bad_deployment in `portfolio-service`, triggered by `dep-5905` (confidence 0.88, high)

### H1: New portfolio-service unindexed trades query saturates shared ledger-db, starving order-service
portfolio-service v4.5.0 (dep-5905, rollout complete 08:56:32Z, status live) began issuing a new unindexed reporting query on the shared ledger-db table 'trades' (SELECT ... WHERE account_id + executed_at ORDER BY executed_at). This executes as a Seq Scan over ~48M rows, driving ledger-db CPU to ~95% and connections_active from ~60 to ~185-197. Because ledger-db is shared, order-service's INSERT INTO orders statements queue behind the load and hit statement timeouts; order-service itself was never changed.

**Causal chain**
1. dep-5905 deployed to portfolio-service, rollout complete 08:56:32Z, adding a new query on trades filtered by account_id + executed_at [F1, F15, F13, F21]
2. New query served as Seq Scan over the 48M-row trades table; first ledger-db slow-query WARN is 08:58:38Z with application_name=portfolio-service, and no such Seq Scan exists anywhere earlier in the window [F11, F15, F20]
3. ledger-db resource step occurs in the 08:58Z 60s bucket (slow_queries 1.33->139.17/min, connections 60.10->185.11, cpu 32.14->95.62), with 08:40-08:57Z verified clean at minute resolution [F19, F9, F10]
4. portfolio-service p99 steps in the identical 08:58 bucket (183ms -> 6964ms) while its error_rate stays flat 0.14-0.27%, i.e. it absorbs the slow queries as latency as a load driver, not a victim emitting 5xx [F22, F23]
5. Deploy completion 08:56:32Z -> Seq Scan/resource step 08:58Z ordering is tight; the earlier 08:48Z onset was a coarse-bucket artifact [F24, F21]
6. order-service INSERT INTO orders statements, arriving at the now-saturated shared DB, time out (statement timeout), first error 09:00:42Z [F8, F4, F17]
7. order-service p99 steps from ~150ms to ~5s at 08:57-09:00Z, matching the DB pressure onset; order-service unchanged (no deploy in window) [F7, F2]
8. ledger-db is shared across order/portfolio/payment/settlement, so no change to order-service is needed to degrade it [F4, F17, F29]
