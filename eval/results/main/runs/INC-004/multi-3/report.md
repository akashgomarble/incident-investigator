# Incident investigation report: INC-004
*System: multi_agent | outcome: **root_cause_identified** | ended: critic_accepted*

## Summary
notification-service's three once-daily OOMKilled restarts (exit 137, 03-21T22:59:50Z / 03-22T21:59:47Z / 03-23T19:59:21Z) are best explained at medium confidence (0.55) by H2: deployment dep-5402 v3.8.0, "Add in-memory template cache for email rendering" (ConcurrentHashMap keyed by template+locale+userSegment). dep-5402 is the only notification-service change in a wide window, was never rolled back, and landed ~23h before the first-ever observed OOMKilled restart, after which heap follows a repeating ~20h ~48%->~98% sawtooth producing exactly one OOM per day with consumer lag spiking only in the OOM buckets [F1,F13,F9,F10,F11,F14,F31,F32,F35,F36]. The reporter's Kafka-replay theory is contradicted: cfg-2410 postdates restarts #1 and #2 by a day each, a retention cut shrinks rather than grows replay demand, and the ramp began ~2 days before the maintenance [F3,F6,F7,F10,F18]. The shared-component alternatives are contradicted, not merely untested: settlement-service, the co-consumer of the same topics, shows flat lag, zero restarts, and no sawtooth in the exact OOM buckets, kafka shows zero under-replicated partitions, and order-service shows zero restarts [F25,F26,F27,F28,F29]. The one load-bearing hop — that TemplateCache has no eviction/TTL/size cap — is UNVERIFIED and known to be unverifiable in this toolset, so this is released as "best supported," not "proven" [F19,F20,F22,F23,F24].

## Probable root cause
**dep-5402 v3.8.0 in-memory TemplateCache drives gradual heap growth to OOM (mechanism unverified)** -- bad_deployment in `notification-service`, triggered by `dep-5402` (confidence 0.55, medium)
